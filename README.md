# 🍽️ Zomato Restaurant Analytics — Business Intelligence Dashboard

A comprehensive **Business Intelligence project** analyzing **9,551 restaurants** across **15 countries and 141 cities** using data from the Zomato platform. Built interactive dashboards in **Excel, Power BI, and Tableau** to uncover customer preferences, pricing trends, cuisine popularity, and service gaps — transforming raw data into actionable business insights.

---

## 📌 Project Highlights

- **9,551 restaurants** analyzed across **141 cities** in **15 countries**
- **~1.5 million customer votes** explored for engagement patterns
- Dashboards built in **3 tools** — Excel, Power BI & Tableau
- End-to-end pipeline: **Data Cleaning → Modeling → DAX Measures → Interactive Visualizations**

---

## 🎯 Objectives

- Study restaurant performance across different countries and cities
- Analyze customer ratings, reviews, and voting patterns
- Compare pricing levels and cuisine preferences
- Identify KPIs that measure restaurant success
- Discover insights to support data-driven decision-making

## 📊 Dashboards

### 1. Excel Dashboard
- **Power Query** for ETL — cleaning, transforming, and merging relational data
- **Star Schema Data Model** using Power Pivot (Restaurant, Cuisine, Rating tables)
- **DAX Measures** — Average Ratings, Total Votes, Cuisine Footprints
- **Cross-filtered Slicers** — Country, City, Cuisine, Price Range
- **PivotTable Drill-downs** — Country → City → Restaurant

### 2. Power BI Dashboard
- Relational data model with Power Query transformations
- DAX measures for Avg Rating, Total Votes, Cost Trends, Cuisine Count
- **Visuals:** KPI Cards, Tree Map, Bar Charts, Line Charts, Donut Charts
- **Drill-down capability** — Country → City → Restaurant level

### 3. Tableau Dashboard
- Fixed-size tiled layout with custom background theme
- Dropdown filters for Country and Cuisine
- **Visuals:** Global Bubble Map, Donut Charts (Delivery & Booking %), Rating Distribution, Cost Analysis, Financial Quarter Breakdown

---

## 📈 Key Findings

- **New Delhi** is the largest food hub — **5,473 restaurants** (57% of dataset)
- **North Indian** cuisine is the most popular — 935 restaurants
- **Online Delivery** available in only **25.66%** of restaurants — huge growth opportunity
- **Table Booking** available in only **12.12%** — digital adoption gap exists
- **89% of restaurants** fall in the budget-friendly tier ($0–$20 for two)
- Most expensive cuisine: **French** (~$438 avg cost for two)
- Many restaurants remain **unrated or underperforming** — quality compliance needed

---

## 💡 Business Recommendations

- Expand **online delivery** and **table booking** features — currently underutilized
- Focus marketing on **budget-friendly restaurants** — the dominant customer segment
- Invest in **quality improvement** programs for unrated/low-rated restaurants
- Leverage **New Delhi's dominance** for pilot programs and A/B testing
- Promote **diverse cuisines** in cities currently dominated by a single cuisine type
